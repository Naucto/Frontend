The icons that stand alone: the top bar, the rail, an empty state. Each is drawn for a box of
twelve cells by twelve, three pixels a cell, so `nc-icon` draws it at 36px and never scales it.

The format is that of icons-24.txt. A name followed by `design` or `pixelarticons` is that set's
glyph, cell for cell: the Naucto Redesign's own, or one of Pixelarticons 2.4.1 by Gerrit Halfmann
(MIT, github.com/halfmage/pixelarticons). A name on its own is drawn for the kit.
